## Fidelity boundary

The public material provides reference sections, product cutaways and opening diagrams, rather than the full fabrication catalogue or DXFs. Published section faces and distinctive components inform the models. Unpublished walls, chamber arrangements, reinforcement ribs, hardware sizes, three-track frame expansion and drain perforations are reconstructed illustrations. They must not be described as manufacturing-exact replicas.

In cutaway view the exterior remains black and the sawn ends and chamber interiors expose raw aluminium. The corner is a small section specimen, so its location at ground level is independent of the recessed installation represented in the full Infinity bay. Chambers are real openings with side walls, not painted black patches.

The selected configurations demonstrate catalogue options; a three/four-leaf example does not imply the system is limited to that configuration. All capacities remain subject to the configuration and hardware conditions already stated in the page.
